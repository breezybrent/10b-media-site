how i work

four things i actually believe.

  1. 01

    views are rented. trust is owned.

    a feed can be rented tomorrow and gone by next quarter. the brand that sounds like itself keeps the audience it built.

  2. 02

    one decision-maker beats a million scrollers.

    reach is a vanity metric unless the right person is watching. i optimize for the one viewer who signs.

  3. 03

    ai should remove the boring parts.

    i build workflows that kill the repetitive production work so the team can spend its hours on the idea.

  4. 04

    if the date is impossible, say so on day one.

    a no you can plan around beats a yes that misses. i would rather lose the pitch than hand you a bad cut.

tell me the number you have to move.

if it is a launch, a rebrand, or a channel that flatlined, i will tell you honestly whether i can move it.
